PRATHYUSHA ENGINEERING COLLEGE
STUDENT DRESS CODE

==================================================
GENERAL DRESS CODE
==================================================

Students must maintain a clean, neat, and well-maintained appearance.

Students should:

- Wear clean and well-maintained clothing.
- Choose comfortable and appropriate footwear.
- Maintain a neat and professional appearance.
- Ensure that clothing is suitable for classrooms, laboratories, and campus activities.

==================================================
MONDAY TO WEDNESDAY – FORMAL DRESS CODE
==================================================

Students must come to college in formal attire from Monday to Wednesday.

GIRLS:

- Salwar kameez is allowed.
- Wearing a shawl is mandatory.
- Leggings are not allowed.
- Jeans pants are not allowed.

BOYS:

- Formal shirts must be worn.
- Shirts should be neatly tucked in.
- Neat formal pants must be worn.

==================================================
PRESENTATIONS AND COLLEGE EVENTS
==================================================

During presentations and other college events, students should wear formal attire.

Formal attire may include:

- Blazers
- Formal pants
- Other appropriate formal clothing

Students should maintain a neat and professional appearance during such events.

==================================================
THURSDAY TO SATURDAY – CASUAL DRESS CODE
==================================================

Students are allowed to wear appropriate casual clothing from Thursday to Saturday.

GIRLS:

The following are allowed:

- Jeans with decent and well-fitted tops
- Kurta
- Shirt
- Long tops
- Modest casual wear

Students are encouraged to wear decent, neat, and appropriate casual clothing.

BOYS:

The following are allowed:

- T-shirts
- Decent shirts
- Casual trousers
- Collared T-shirts
- Other appropriate casual clothing

Appropriate footwear should be worn.

==================================================
CLOTHING NOT ALLOWED
==================================================

The following clothing is not allowed on campus:

- Ripped jeans
- Torn jeans
- Heavily distressed jeans
- Cropped tops
- Very short tops
- Transparent tops
- See-through clothing
- Revealing outfits
- Very short skirts
- Short pants
- Party wear
- Beach wear

==================================================
SUMMARY
==================================================

Monday to Wednesday:
Formal dress code is mandatory.

Thursday to Saturday:
Appropriate casual dress is allowed.

All students must maintain a neat, decent, professional, and appropriate appearance suitable for college activities.
