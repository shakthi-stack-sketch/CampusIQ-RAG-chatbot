PRATHYUSHA ENGINEERING COLLEGE
CLUBS, INNOVATION AND STUDENT DEVELOPMENT

STUDENT DEVELOPMENT

Students are encouraged to develop skills beyond regular academics.

The college provides opportunities for students to participate in clubs, technical activities, workshops, seminars, projects, hackathons and extracurricular activities.

Students are encouraged to improve their:

• Technical skills
• Coding skills
• Communication skills
• Public speaking skills
• Presentation skills
• Practical knowledge
• Teamwork
• Innovation skills

TECHNICAL AND STUDENT CLUBS

The college provides opportunities for students to participate in various clubs and development activities, including:

• Google Developer Club
• Digital Marketing Club
• Gaming Club
• AWS Club
• ACM Club
• PACE Club
• PALS Club
• Maths Club
• Drones Club
• Yantramanav
• Robotics-related training and activities
• AR-VR Center of Excellence

Students also participate in workshops, seminars, technical training programmes and other extracurricular activities.

HACKATHONS

Hackathons are conducted to encourage innovation and practical problem-solving.

Different departments conduct hackathons and students are encouraged to participate.

Students may also be taken to different locations for hackathon participation, with support and motivation provided by the college.

INNOVATION DOMAINS

Students are divided into and learn through different innovation domains.

The 13 Domains of Innovation include:

1. Agriculture
2. Disaster Management
3. Drones & Defence
4. E-Governance
5. Education
6. FinTech
7. Healthcare 01 – Wearable Devices
8. Healthcare 02 – MedTech
9. Industry 4.0 & 5.0
10. Mobility & Transportation
11. Renewable Energy
12. Robotics & Automation
13. Smart & Urban Technology

These domains provide students with opportunities to explore different technology areas, develop innovative ideas and work on practical projects.
