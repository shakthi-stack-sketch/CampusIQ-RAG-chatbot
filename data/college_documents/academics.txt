PRATHYUSHA ENGINEERING COLLEGE
ACADEMIC INFORMATION

ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

The undergraduate engineering programme follows a four-year academic structure consisting of eight semesters.

Each academic year contains two semesters.

ASSESSMENTS AND EXAMINATIONS

During each semester, students undergo:

• Three Internal Assessment Tests (IAT)
• One Model Examination
• One Model Practical Examination
• Practical Examination
• Theory End Semester Examination

Students also undergo End Semester Preparatory Classes, also referred to as University Preparation Classes, before their university examinations.

SYLLABUS COMPLETION

The syllabus for every course is planned to be completed within the allotted academic schedule.

Faculty members are responsible for completing the prescribed portions for their respective subjects within the available time.

COURSE STRUCTURE

Each subject or course consists of five lessons or units.

Courses are designed and maintained with consideration for industry requirements and relevant academic standards.

PRACTICAL AND SKILL-BASED LEARNING

Students are encouraged to focus not only on theoretical academics but also on practical learning and skill development.

Students participate in:

• Coding practice
• Projects
• Workshops
• Seminars
• Hackathons
• Internship opportunities
• Technical training
• Industry-oriented learning activities

CODING AND TECHNICAL TRAINING

Students receive opportunities for coding and technical skill development.

Training activities include coding practice through platforms such as:

• LeetCode
• HackerRank

Students are encouraged to strengthen their programming and problem-solving skills alongside their academic coursework.

PRESENTATION SKILLS

During each semester, students participate in two group presentations.

These presentations help students improve:

• Public speaking skills
• Communication skills
• Presentation skills
• Team collaboration

PROJECT-BASED LEARNING

Students work on projects as part of their learning experience.

The academic environment encourages students to apply theoretical knowledge through practical projects and technical activities.

INTERNSHIPS AND CAREER DEVELOPMENT

Students are provided opportunities for:

• Workshops
• Internship opportunities
• Technical training
• Coding training
• Skill development activities

The academic approach focuses on helping students develop both subject knowledge and practical industry-relevant skills.
