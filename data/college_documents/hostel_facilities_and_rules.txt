PRATHYUSHA ENGINEERING COLLEGE
HOSTEL FACILITIES AND RULES

HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION

Hostel rooms accommodate approximately two to three students.

Students are provided with basic furniture and facilities including:

• Individual cot
• Cupboard
• Study table
• Light
• Fan

The hostel rooms are maintained to be clean, well-painted and well-maintained.

PERSONAL REQUIREMENTS

Students are responsible for bringing or purchasing certain personal requirements.

These may include:

• Bucket
• Jug
• Water bottle
• Chair
• Other personal necessities

Students may bring these items themselves or purchase required items during the college admission period if available.

HOSTEL LEAVE PROCEDURE

Hostel students who wish to go home must obtain the required approvals.

Students need signatures from:

• Mentor
• Head of Department (HOD)
• Warden

After obtaining the required signatures, students submit their leave card at the security gate before leaving.

Leave card information:

• Boys use a Pink Card
• Girls use a Yellow Card

The card is submitted at the security gate when leaving the college.

RESTROOM AND WASHING FACILITIES

Hostel restroom facilities are available.

Hot water facilities are available for washing clothes.

A backyard area is provided for students to wash clothes.

However, washing machines are not available.

OTHER HOSTEL AND CAMPUS FACILITIES

Available facilities include:

• Drinking water facilities
• Wi-Fi facilities
• Laboratory facilities
• Gym facilities

Students can access these facilities as provided by the college and hostel environment.
